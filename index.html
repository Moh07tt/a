Sources & methodology

How this public intelligence dashboard handles information.

Connected dataCISA KEV is retrieved from its public JSON feed when the page loads. The dashboard shows the source status and refresh time.
External referencesNVD, MITRE ATT&CK and Iraqi resources currently open their publishers' websites; they are not imported into this dashboard.
Editorial ruleA public advisory is not proof of a local incident. Every future report should include its publisher, publication date and original link.
